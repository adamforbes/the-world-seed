Era Mascots

One for each Era, beside its heading, built on its suit.

Myth ♥

Idle
A heart on bird legs wanders back and forth, beating.
Hover
It stops, turns to watch you, and beats faster.
Click
It buds: a little heart pops off and toddles away.

Antiquity ♦

Idle
A sage whose head is a cut diamond, holding a scroll. Its facets shimmer.
Hover
He raises the scroll and the gem blazes.
Click
He unrolls the scroll; its runes rise, circle his head and sink into the gem, which flares.

Ruin ♣

Idle
A bald, muscled woman in black leather with a black cudgel on her shoulder.
Hover
She flexes.
Click
She slams the club on her own head: a frozen, shaking hit with a flash, then she's pumped, red-eyed and steaming, in a double flex. Click again to chain hits.

Promise ♠

Idle
A tall, gaunt cormorant with a pale stare, ♠ marks in its feathers and a gold coin in its bill.
Hover
Skittish of the pointer: it leans away and trembles when it gets close.
Click
It leaps and flaps hard, tossing the coin up spinning, and catches it again.

Margin Drolleries

One beside each section they illustrate.

Places

Idle
A mango tree stands still; now and then a leaf drifts down.
Hover
Its leaves rustle.
Click
It shakes and a golden mango drops off its stalk. The mangos pile up.

Gods

Idle

Hover
The eye follows your pointer.
Click

Peoples

Idle
One hops, one taps a foot, one's hat tip bobs.
Hover

Click

Actors

Idle
The snail inches. The pennant flutters.
Hover
The snail's eyestalks follow you.
Click
The knight lifts their helm: also a snail.

Nations

Idle
The flag hangs limp, stirring now and then.
Hover
A gust snaps it out.
Click
Lightning strikes the end of the flag, which burns fast back to the pole; a new flag goes up.

Qualities & Relationships

Idle
A great figure's portrait hangs in a gilt frame, a plaque beneath naming them Wise. They watch you, and blink now and then.
Hover
Their eyes follow your pointer.
Click
The frame swings and a brush repaints them top to bottom as the plaque's word is struck through and replaced: the Wise king, the Holy saint, the Cruel tyrant, then the tyrant's portrait defaced, Fallen.

Naming

Hover
It cries and waves its arms, shaking its rattle.
Click
It calms, a gold star appears, and it sleeps.

Journey

Hover

Click

Narration

Idle

Hover

Draw a Card

Idle
The card floats and turns a little in its own glow, sparks winking round it.
Hover
A corner lifts to peek.
Click
It spins up into the air and lands face up in a burst of rays, a ring and flying pips. Again, and it turns back down.

Legacy

Idle
The statue stands; its gold glints.
Hover
A pigeon circles overhead.
Click
The statue breaks into chunks under the pigeon. Click the rubble: a statue of the pigeon.
