Rules

Era Cards

Reminder on Qualities If a card doesn't say how many Qualities to give, a Named Entity gets one of your choice, plus any the card adds (Ancient, "a positive Quality"). If it does ("two Qualities"), that's the total.

Reminder on Journeys Short: 2 tasks, 1 outcome. Medium: 3 tasks, 2 outcomes. Long: 4 tasks, 3 outcomes. Epic: 5 tasks, 4 outcomes.

Reminder on ending in Failure Journeys that end in Failure still get their full number of outcomes; however, at least half the outcomes must be negative (e.g. remove instead of add, negative Quality instead of positive).

♥ Era of Myth

2♥

Two of HeartsPrimal Genesis

The Gods dream, and the dream takes shape. What is born, and who feels it? Choose one:

  • Name a natural Place and give it the Quality Uncharted.
  • Name a God and give it the Quality Awakened.
  • Name a People and give it the Quality Newborn.

Then Narrate the event.

3♥

Three of HeartsDivine Whispers

Choose one:

  • A God speaks to a People. What do they say?
  • A People prays to a God. What do they ask for?

Give the People a Relationship to the God (e.g. Worships [God], Chosen by [God], Cursed by [God]). Then Narrate what comes of it, or give a Journey that follows from it.

4♥

Four of HeartsA People Remade

Like rivers, Peoples split apart and flow together. What divides them, or what draws them together? Choose one:

  • A People fractures, and its dissenters become a new People.
  • Two Peoples merge into a new People.

Name the new People and give it a Relationship to its source (e.g. Split from [People], Exiled from [People], Union of [People] and [People]). The source Peoples remain, and each gains or loses a Quality.

5♥

Five of HeartsScion of the Gods

An Actor is born to a God. Name an Actor, give them the Relationship Child of [God] and give them two Qualities, one positive and one negative, then describe their relation to their divine parent.

6♥

Six of HeartsFire of the Gods

Choose one:

  • A God gives a People or Actor a gift. What do they give? Narrate a related event.
  • A People or Actor tries to steal from a God. What do they try to steal? Give a related Journey.
7♥

Seven of HeartsThe One Piece

Choose one:

  • A Place is discovered to be abundant in resources. What is the resource?
  • A God designates a Place as sacred. Which God? Why?

Name the Place and give it a positive Quality. Give a People a Journey to migrate to the area.

8♥

Eight of HeartsTremble!

A creature of significant presence is born into the world. What does it hunt, guard or seek? Name a creature Actor and give it two Qualities. Choose one:

  • Its arrival reshapes a Place or affects a People. Narrate an event.
  • It has a unique purpose. Give it a Journey to fulfill this goal.
9♥

Nine of HeartsThe Darklord

A villain rises to terrorize the world. Name or select an Actor and give them two Qualities, both negative.

10♥

Ten of HeartsGames of the Gods

Choose one:

  • Two Gods declare opposition to one another. What is the source of their conflict (e.g. betrayal, insult, romantic rivalry)? Each God gains the Relationship Rival of [God]. Narrate their first conflict.
  • Two Gods make a wager, with the fate of their followers as the stakes. What are the terms? For each God, give a People who worships it a medium Journey. If a God has no worshippers, a People first gains Worships [God]. The first Journey to complete succeeds, and the other fails.
J♥

Jack of HeartsThe Great Catalyst

A powerful magic event unfolds in an instant. What is it? Where does it occur? Narrate the event. You make the first response as well as the last.

Q♥

Queen of HeartsHeaven and Earth

Choose one:

  • A mortal ascends to godhood. How? What People did they come from? Name a God, or turn an existing Actor into a God. It keeps its Qualities and Relationships and gains Ascended.
  • A God descends to become an Actor. Why? What happens to their power? Name an Actor who was once a God, or turn an existing God into an Actor. It keeps its Qualities and Relationships and gains Fallen. Like any Actor, it dies at the end of the Era.
K♥

King of HeartsThe First Banner

A People's first formal government is established. Who rules, and by what right? Who is left out? Name a Nation for that People. Give the Nation the Quality Ancient.

A♥

Ace of HeartsA World, Alive!

The canvas of the world unfurls as the Gods shape it to their will. Name three natural Places. Give each a thematically linked Quality or Relationship.

♦ Era of Antiquity

2♦

Two of DiamondsThe Drums of War

Two Peoples or Nations go to war. Who are they? What are they fighting over? Give them both a medium or longer Journey to win the war. The first Journey to succeed causes the other to end in failure.

3♦

Three of DiamondsThe Word of God

A People's faith in a God is codified into a holy text. Choose one:

  • The text unites the faithful. Name a Nation and give it the Quality Pious, Devout, or Zealous.
  • The text divides the faithful. Choose a devout People: they gain the Quality Orthodox. Then, create a new People from their dissenters, who gain the Quality Heretical and the Relationship Split from [People].
4♦

Four of DiamondsDreams of a Better Life

Choose one:

  • A People prosper. Name a Nation populated by the People, then give it the Quality Prosperous and another positive Quality.
  • The lure of a better life draws Peoples to a Nation. Give two Peoples a Journey to migrate to it.
5♦

Five of DiamondsOutlanders

People from somewhere far away arrive (e.g. another realm, space, hell). How? Was it intentional? Name a People and give them two Qualities, one positive and one negative.

6♦

Six of DiamondsAncient Bonds

The ancient world teems with powerful creatures, offering both companionship and new frontiers. Choose one:

  • An Actor or People aim to create a bond with a creature. Name a creature Actor, and give the Actor or People a Journey to forge the bond.
  • A creature seeks to make a new home. Name the creature Actor, and describe the creature's Journey to find or create this home, and how its presence transforms the Place.
7♦

Seven of DiamondsStrong Winds

The times are kind and the winds are fair. Choose one:

  • Remove 1 task from all Journeys.
  • Give three Entities a positive Quality.
8♦

Eight of DiamondsA New World

A new world is found! Name a large Place and give it two Qualities, one positive and one negative. Then give a People or Nation a medium or longer Journey to claim it.

9♦

Nine of DiamondsUnited in Glory

Two Actors, Peoples, or Nations join forces to accomplish a goal. What is it? Give them each a single medium or longer Journey to accomplish it.

10♦

Ten of DiamondsDoom

Give a People or Nation the Quality Doomed. At the end of the Era remove this Entity from the game. All Nations and Places populated with these peoples get the Relationship Former Home of [Entity]. When it is removed, it can leave a Legacy as if it were a departing Actor. This Legacy doesn't count toward any player's Legacy choice.

J♦

Jack of DiamondsAge of Wonder

Nations compete to build lasting monuments to their glory. Choose up to three Nations and give them a Journey to construct an architectural wonder.

Q♦

Queen of DiamondsMicro/Macro History

Choose one:

  • It is a time of great Peoples. Choose two Peoples and Name an Actor from each. Give them each two Qualities, one positive and one negative.
  • It is a time of great Nations. Choose two Peoples and Name a Nation from each. Give them each two Qualities, one positive and one negative.
K♦

King of DiamondsImmortal!

A great, immortal Actor hero rises. Name or select an Actor and give them the Quality Immortal and a positive Quality. This Actor does not die when the Era changes.

A♦

Ace of DiamondsProgress For Some

Choose one:

  • It is a time for great Journeys. Give two Journeys whose aim is to discover, invent, or spread peace.
  • Great Journeys are accomplished. Choose two Journeys and immediately complete them successfully.

♣ Era of Ruin

2♣

Two of ClubsTarnished Legacy

Choose one:

  • The line of a former hero betrays their legacy. Name an Actor descended from a Myth or Antiquity era Actor with two negative Qualities and the Relationship Descendant of [Actor].
  • Return any Actor from a prior age. Reverse all of their positive Qualities to negative Qualities. Give them the Quality Returned.
3♣

Three of ClubsForsaken

Abandon all hope. Give a God or People the Quality Forsaken. All Journeys on them end in failure.

  • If a God, the world turns its back on it. Remove all Relationships between the God and other Entities. It cannot affect the world until it gains a new Relationship.
  • If a People, the Nations they live in cast them out. Each of those Nations gains the Relationship Exiled [People], and the People loses its Relationships to those Nations.
4♣

Four of ClubsDisaster

A natural disaster occurs. Choose an existing Place to give two negative Qualities. For each Nation in that location, if it has two or fewer positive Qualities, remove it from play, otherwise remove a positive Quality and add a negative Quality to it.

5♣

Five of ClubsMartyrdom

An Actor is martyred for a greater cause. The Actor with the most positive Qualities is removed from play. Every People, Nation, and God aligned with them replaces a negative Quality with positive and any Journeys they have complete successfully. What is the cause? What are they remembered for?

6♣

Six of ClubsAnnexation

A Nation sets out to conquer its neighbor, and some rise to resist. Give a Nation a medium or longer Journey to conquer another Nation. Name a group Actor with one Quality and the Relationship Enemy of [conquering Nation]. If the Journey succeeds, remove the conquered Nation from play and add its territory to the conqueror, in addition to the other outcomes. The Group endures.

7♣

Seven of ClubsDead Calm

Choose one:

  • The world stalls. Add 2 tasks to every Journey in progress.
  • A Journey ends in catastrophe. Choose a Journey in progress. It completes now in failure, with 1 more outcome than usual, and every outcome must be negative.
8♣

Eight of ClubsInsurrection

A coup is plotted. Why? Either name or select an Actor within a Nation. Give the Nation a negative Quality. Give the Actor the Quality Revolutionary and give them a long or epic Journey to overthrow the Nation. If they succeed, remove the Nation from play at the end of the Journey in addition to other outcomes.

9♣

Nine of ClubsConflict

Choose one:

  • Two Actors go to battle. Why are they fighting? Give both a Journey to defeat the other. Give the winner the Relationship Bested [Actor]. The first Journey to succeed causes the other to end in failure.
  • A Place suddenly becomes hostile. Give it a negative Quality, then Narrate a related event.
10♣

Ten of ClubsCollapse

A great Nation of the world collapses. Give it the quality Collapsed and remove it from play. Narrate an Event about its collapse.

J♣

Jack of ClubsSpreading Corruption

Choose one:

  • A hero falters and gives in to darkness. Choose an Actor with more positive Qualities than negatives and replace all of their positives with negatives.
  • A villain realizes their desires. Choose any Actor with more negative Qualities than positives. Choose a related Journey to this Actor and it completes in success. The outcomes must be negative towards other Entities.
Q♣

Queen of ClubsLeft to the Wolves

It is a dark time for those without structure. Add two negative Qualities to each People without at least one Nation.

K♣

King of ClubsImmortal...

An immortal villain rises. Name or select an Actor and give them the Quality Immortal and a negative Quality. This Actor does not die when the Era changes.

A♣

Ace of ClubsRoad to Ruin

Choose one:

  • It is a time for dire Journeys. Give two Journeys whose aim is to subjugate, destroy, or spread violence.
  • Great Journeys fail. Choose two Journeys; they immediately end in failure. (At least half the outcomes must be negative.)

♠ Era of Promise

2♠

Two of SpadesWild Magic

Magic slips its bounds. Choose a Place or Nation and narrate the strange magical event that happens there. Then give it a Quality that reflects the event.

3♠

Three of SpadesCall to Adventure

A Nation calls for heroes. What threatens them, and what reward do they promise? Name or select an Actor who answers the call. Give them the Relationship Champion of [Nation] and a medium or longer Journey to face the threat.

4♠

Four of SpadesThe Faithful

A God calls out to the world. Which God? What do they promise those who answer? Name or select an Actor or a Nation. Give them a Relationship to this God (e.g. Cult of [God], Worships [God], Chosen by [God]). Give them a medium or longer Journey to follow their God's desires.

5♠

Five of SpadesMagnum Opus

A work of creative genius sweeps the world with its popularity. What is it? Who made it, and why? Narrate an event it inspires, affecting up to three Entities who are moved by it.

6♠

Six of SpadesBreakthrough

Choose one:

  • A novel discovery is made in a Nation. What do they discover? Was this the product of effort, or was it an accident? Narrate a related event affecting the Nation and all Peoples and Actors within it.
  • A Nation's economic balance shifts dramatically. What caused this? Who benefits and who loses? Narrate an event that affects the Nation and all Peoples and Actors within it.
7♠

Seven of SpadesBittersweet

One Journey ends, another loses ground. Choose a Journey to complete immediately, then add 3 tasks to another Journey.

8♠

Eight of SpadesGilded Age

Choose one:

  • Two Nations begin a prosperous trade relationship. What do they trade? Give both Nations the Relationship Trading with [Nation]. Name an Actor who seeks to protect or plunder it.
  • The few grow rich at the expense of the many. Name an Actor within a Nation and give them the Quality Wealthy. Give the Nation the Relationship Extracted by [Actor].
9♠

Nine of SpadesHunted

Choose one:

  • A Nation turns on a People within its borders. Who do they blame, and for what? Give the People the Relationship Persecuted by [Nation]. Give the Nation a medium or longer Journey to drive the People out. If it succeeds, the People no longer live in the Nation.
  • A large bounty is set. Name or select an Actor and give them the Quality Wanted and a negative Quality. Give another Actor a Journey to slay or capture them.
10♠

Ten of SpadesDigging Up the Past

Someone looks to the past. What are they searching for, and why now? Choose an Actor or Nation to be the seeker, and an Entity from a prior Era or one removed from play. Give the seeker the Relationship Seeking [Entity] and a medium or longer Journey to find it.

J♠

Jack of SpadesGuest Right

Choose one:

  • A Nation becomes open and multicultural. Add two Peoples not currently present in a Nation to that Nation. Give the Nation the Quality Multicultural and a positive Quality. Give each of the Peoples a positive Quality.
  • Old wounds heal. Choose two Entities with a negative Relationship to each other (e.g. Enemy of [Nation], Rival of [God], Persecuted by [Nation]). Replace it with a positive Relationship.
Q♠

Queen of SpadesBig Talker

An Actor makes a public vow to do the impossible. What do they swear to achieve, and who heard them? Give them the Quality Daring and an epic Journey to fulfil their vow.

K♠

King of SpadesThe Lost Heir

An heir to a fallen Nation steps forward. Choose a Nation removed from play. Name or select an Actor and give them the Relationship Heir of [Nation] and a long or epic Journey to reclaim it. If they succeed, return the Nation to play in addition to the other outcomes.

A♠

Ace of SpadesOnce Upon a Time

The time of the Tale is beginning! Draw and resolve one more card, then the game ends and the adventure begins. Journeys still in progress do not complete. They become the open threads of your Tale, waiting for heroes to finish them.

★ Extra Cards

The following are extra cards that may appear in any Era.

🃏

Black Joker

Fate repeats itself. Replay the previously played Era card. If the card presented a choice of options, you must choose the unchosen one.

🃏

Red Joker

The winds of fate blow in a random direction. Take another action. You may take an action normally not allowed in this Era.
