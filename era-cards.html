Rules

Era Cards

Reminder on Qualities If a card doesn't say how many Qualities to give, a Named Entity gets one of your choice, plus any the card adds (Ancient, "a positive Quality"). If it does ("two Qualities"), that's the total.

Reminder on Journeys Short: 2 tasks, 1 outcome. Medium: 3 tasks, 2 outcomes. Long: 4 tasks, 3 outcomes. Epic: 5 tasks, 4 outcomes.

Reminder on ending in Failure Journeys that end in Failure still get their full number of outcomes; however, at least half the outcomes must be negative (e.g. remove instead of add, negative Quality instead of positive).

Trim the cards over 70 words, the style guide's soft limit (the deck's median is about 50): 9♠ Hunted, 10♥ Games of the Gods, Q♥ Heaven and Earth, 6♦ Ancient Bonds, 8♣ Insurrection, J♠ Guest Right, 7♠ Fellowship, 4♦ Prosperity, 3♦ The Word of God and 4♥ A People Remade.

♥ Era of Myth

2♥

Two of HeartsPrimal Genesis

The Gods dream, and the dream takes shape. What is born, and who feels it? Choose one:

  • Name a natural Place and give it the Quality Uncharted.
  • Name a God and give it the Quality Awakened.
  • Name a People and give them the Quality Newborn.

Then Narrate the event.

3♥

Three of HeartsDivine Whispers

Choose one:

  • A God speaks to a People. What does it say?
  • A People prays to a God. What do they ask for?

Give the People a Relationship to the God (e.g. Worships [God], Chosen by [God], Cursed by [God]). Then Narrate what comes of it, or give a Journey that follows from it.

4♥

Four of HeartsA People Remade

Like rivers, Peoples split apart and flow together. What divides them, or what draws them together? Choose one:

  • A People fractures, and their dissenters become a new People.
  • Two Peoples merge into a new People.

Name the new People and give them a Relationship to their source (e.g. Split from [People], Exiled from [People], Union of [People] and [People]). The source Peoples remain, and each gains or loses a Quality.

5♥

Five of HeartsScion of the Gods

An Actor is born to a God. Name an Actor, give them the Relationship Child of [God] and give them two Qualities, one positive and one negative, then describe their relation to their divine parent.

6♥

Six of HeartsFire of the Gods

Choose one:

  • A God gives a People or Actor a gift. What does it give? Narrate a related event.
  • A People or Actor tries to steal from a God. What do they try to steal? Give a related Journey.
7♥

Seven of HeartsThe One Piece

Choose one:

  • A Place is discovered to be abundant in resources. What is the resource?
  • A God designates a Place as sacred. Which God? Why?

Name the Place and give it a positive Quality. Give a People a Journey to migrate to the area.

8♥

Eight of HeartsTremble!

A creature of significant presence is born into the world. What does it hunt, guard or seek? Name a creature Actor and give it two Qualities. Choose one:

  • Its arrival reshapes a Place or affects a People. Narrate an event.
  • It has a unique purpose. Give it a Journey to fulfill this goal.
9♥

Nine of HeartsThe Darklord

A villain rises to terrorize the world. Name or select an Actor and give them two Qualities, both negative.

10♥

Ten of HeartsGames of the Gods

Choose one:

  • Two Gods declare opposition to one another. What is the source of their conflict (e.g. betrayal, insult, romantic rivalry)? Each God gains the Relationship Rival of [God]. Narrate their first conflict.
  • Two Gods make a wager, with the fate of their followers as the stakes. What are the terms? For each God, give a People who worships it a medium Journey. If a God has no worshippers, a People first gains the Relationship Worships [God]. The first Journey to complete succeeds, and the other fails.
J♥

Jack of HeartsThe Great Catalyst

A powerful magic event unfolds in an instant. What is it? Where does it occur? Narrate the event. You make the first response as well as the last.

Q♥

Queen of HeartsHeaven and Earth

Choose one:

  • A mortal ascends to godhood. How? What People did they come from? Name a God, or turn an existing Actor into a God. It keeps its Qualities and Relationships and gains the Quality Ascended.
  • A God descends to become an Actor. Why? What happens to its power? Name an Actor who was once a God, or turn an existing God into an Actor. They keep their Qualities and Relationships and gain the Quality Fallen. Like any Actor, they die at the end of the Era.
K♥

King of HeartsThe First Banner

A People's first formal government is established. Who rules, and by what right? Who is left out? Name a Nation for that People. Give the Nation the Quality Ancient.

A♥

Ace of HeartsA World, Alive!

The canvas of the world unfurls as the Gods shape it to their will. Name three natural Places. Give each a thematically linked Quality or Relationship.

♦ Era of Antiquity

2♦

Two of DiamondsThe Drums of War

Two Peoples or Nations go to war. Who are they? What are they fighting over? Give each a Journey of the same size to win the war, and the Relationship At war with [Entity]. The first Journey to complete succeeds, and the other fails.

3♦

Three of DiamondsThe Word of God

A People's faith in a God is codified into a holy text. What does it say? Choose one:

  • The text unites the faithful. Name a Nation of that People and give it the Quality Pious, Devout, or Zealous and the Relationship Worships [God].
  • The text divides the faithful. Select two Peoples or Nations that share the faith. One gains the Quality Orthodox, the other Heretical, and each gains the Relationship Enemy of [Entity].
4♦

Four of DiamondsProsperity

Choose one:

  • A People prosper. What makes them rich? Does everyone share in it? Name a Nation populated by the People, then give it the Quality Prosperous and another positive Quality.
  • The lure of a better life draws a People to a Nation. What do they hope to find? Give the Nation a positive Quality that shows it (e.g. Fertile, Peaceful, Tolerant), and give the People a Journey to migrate to it.
5♦

Five of DiamondsOutlanders

People from somewhere far away arrive (e.g. beyond the sea, the fires below, the stars). How did they get here, and who meets them first? Name a People and give them two Qualities (e.g. Displaced, Curious, Conquering).

6♦

Six of DiamondsAncient Bonds

Great beasts roam the ancient world. Choose one:

  • A creature of legend chooses a People or Nation. Does it protect them, serve them, or demand tribute? Name a creature Actor and give it a Relationship to them (e.g. Guardian of [People], Servant of [Nation], Feared by [People]).
  • A Nation masters a kind of creature. Which creature, and how does it change the Nation? Give the Nation a Quality that shows it (e.g. Mounted, Winged, Furry). Name an Actor of the Nation who embodies the bond.
7♦

Seven of DiamondsFair Winds

The times are kind and the winds are fair. Choose one:

  • Remove 1 task from all Journeys.
  • Who prospers in these good years? Give three Entities a positive Quality.
8♦

Eight of DiamondsA New World

A new world is found! Name a large Place and give it two Qualities, one positive and one negative. Then give an Actor or Nation a medium or longer Journey to claim it.

9♦

Nine of DiamondsUnited in Glory

Two Peoples, Actors, or Nations join forces to accomplish a goal. What is it? Give each a Journey toward it, and the Relationship Allied with [Entity].

10♦

Ten of DiamondsDoom

Give a People or Nation the Quality Doomed. What dooms them? Do they know? If they still have Doomed at the end of the Era, remove them from play. Every Place and Nation they lived in gains the Relationship Former Home of [Entity], and you leave a Legacy for them as if they were a departing Actor.

J♦

Jack of DiamondsAge of Wonder

Two Nations, or rival factions within one, compete to build lasting monuments to their glory. What will each build? For each wonder, select a Nation and give it a medium or longer Journey to raise it.

Q♦

Queen of DiamondsGiants of the Age

Choose one:

  • It is a time of storied figures. Select two Peoples and Name an Actor from each.
  • It is a time of ambitious flags. Select two Peoples and Name a Nation from each.

What makes them great, and what flaw comes with it? Give each two Qualities, one positive and one negative.

K♦

King of DiamondsImmortal!

An immortal hero rises. What made them immortal, and who or what do they protect? Name or select an Actor and give them the Quality Immortal and a positive Quality. This Actor does not die when the Era changes.

A♦

Ace of DiamondsEureka

Choose one:

  • It is a time for great Journeys. Give two Journeys whose aim is to discover, invent, or spread peace.
  • Great Journeys are accomplished. Select two Journeys in progress. They end now in success.

♣ Era of Ruin

2♣

Two of ClubsTarnished Legacy

Choose one:

  • A hero's line betrays their legacy. Name an Actor descended from an Actor of a prior Era. Give them two negative Qualities and the Relationship Descendant of [Actor]. How do they besmirch their name?
  • A hero of old returns, but not as they were. Select an Actor from a prior Era and reverse their positive Qualities to negative. Give them the Quality Returned. What brought them back?
3♣

Three of ClubsForsaken

Abandon all hope. Give a God or People the Quality Forsaken. Every Journey they have now ends in failure.

  • A God: the world turns its back on it. Remove all its Relationships with other Entities. It cannot affect the world until it gains a new Relationship.
  • A People: the Nations they live in cast them out. Each of those Nations gains the Relationship Exiled [People].

What will they do alone?

4♣

Four of ClubsCataclysm

The earth turns on its people. Select a Place and give it two negative Qualities. Each Nation there with two or fewer positive Qualities is removed from play. The rest swap a positive Quality for a negative one. What was the disaster, and who saw it coming?

5♣

Five of ClubsMartyrdom

An Actor is martyred for a greater cause. The Actor with the most positive Qualities (you choose if tied) is removed from play. Select up to three Entities related to them. Each swaps a negative Quality for a positive one, and one of its Journeys ends now in success. What is the cause? What are they remembered for?

6♣

Six of ClubsAnnexation

A Nation sets out to conquer its neighbor. Why? Give it a medium or longer Journey to conquer another Nation. Name a group Actor to resist it, with the Relationship Enemy of [Nation]. If the Journey succeeds, use 2 of its outcomes to remove the conquered Nation from play, and redraw the conqueror's borders to swallow it. The resistance does not die when the Era changes.

7♣

Seven of ClubsDead Calm

Choose one:

  • The world stalls. Add 2 tasks to every Journey in progress.
  • A Journey ends in catastrophe. Select a Journey in progress. It ends now in failure, with 1 more outcome than usual, and every outcome must be negative.

If no Journey is in progress, shuffle this card into the deck and draw another.

8♣

Eight of ClubsInsurrection

A coup is plotted. Why? Give a Nation a Quality that shows it (e.g. Decadent, Rudderless, Draconian). Name or select an Actor of the Nation and give them a long or epic Journey to overthrow it. If the Journey succeeds, use 2 of its outcomes for one of these:

  • Seize it. Rename the Nation and give it a Quality that shows the new regime (e.g. Tyrannical, Liberated, Purged).
  • Tear it down. Remove the Nation from play.
9♣

Nine of ClubsBloodshed

Choose one:

  • Two Actors go to battle. Why? Give each a Journey of the same size to defeat the other. The first to complete succeeds, and the other fails. In addition to its outcomes, the winner gains the Relationship Bested [Actor].
  • A battle scars the land. Give a Place a negative Quality (e.g. Bloodsoaked, Scorched, Salted) and Narrate the battle fought there.
10♣

Ten of ClubsCollapse

A great Nation collapses. Did its land fail it, its neighbors, or itself? Give a Nation a Quality that shows how it fell (e.g. Barren, Besieged, Blind) and remove it from play. Narrate its fall.

J♣

Jack of ClubsSpreading Corruption

Choose one:

  • Corruption takes hold. How does it spread? Select an Entity and two Entities related to it. Give all three the same negative Quality (e.g. Plagued, Fanatical, Cursed).
  • A villain realizes their desires. Select an Actor with more negative Qualities than positive. One of their Journeys ends now in success, and its outcomes must harm other Entities.
Q♣

Queen of ClubsLeft to the Wolves

It is a dark time for those without structure. Who are the wolves? Give each People that lives in no Nation two negative Qualities. If there are none, select a People to be cast out first: each Nation they live in gains the Relationship Exiled [People].

K♣

King of ClubsImmortal...

An immortal villain rises. What made them immortal, and who or what do they threaten? Name or select an Actor and give them the Quality Immortal and a negative Quality. This Actor does not die when the Era changes.

A♣

Ace of ClubsPath to Exile

Choose one:

  • It is a time for dire Journeys. Give two Journeys whose aim is to subjugate, destroy, or spread violence.
  • Great Journeys fail. Select two Journeys in progress. They end now in failure. (Reminder: a failed Journey still has outcomes, but at least half of them must be negative.)

♠ Era of Promise

2♠

Two of SpadesWild Magic

Magic slips its bounds. What breaks loose? Select a Place or Nation and Narrate the strange event that happens there. Then give it a Quality that shows it (e.g. Haunted, Hidden, Hallowed).

3♠

Three of SpadesThe Faithful

A God calls out to the world, perhaps one long forgotten. Which God? What does it promise those who answer? Name or select an Actor or Nation. Give them a Relationship to the God (e.g. Cult of [God], Worships [God], Chosen by [God]) and a Journey to do its will.

4♠

Four of SpadesCall to Adventure

A Nation calls for heroes. What threatens it, and what reward does it promise? Give the Nation a negative Quality that shows the threat (e.g. Raided, Frozen, Infested). Name or select an Actor who answers the call. Give them the Relationship Champion of [Nation] and a Journey to face the threat.

5♠

Five of SpadesMagnum Opus

A work of genius sweeps the world. What is it? Select the Actor who made it, dead or alive, and give them a Quality that shows what kind of artist they are (e.g. Creative, Obsessed, Tormented). Then Narrate what the work changes: a law, a war, a way of life.

6♠

Six of SpadesBreakthrough

A breakthrough upends what a Nation knows or can do: a new truth, or a new invention. What is it, and who refuses to accept it? Give the Nation a Quality that shows it (e.g. Enlightened, Industrial, Seafaring). Then Narrate the clash between old and new within it.

7♠

Seven of SpadesFellowship

One bearer falls, another takes up the quest. Select a Journey in progress and give it to another Entity, with a Relationship to the old bearer (e.g. Heir of [Actor], Sworn to [Actor], Avenger of [Actor]). The quest proves greater than anyone knew: add 1 task to it, and it ends with 1 more outcome than usual.

If no Journey is in progress, shuffle this card into the deck and draw another.

8♠

Eight of SpadesGilded Age

Choose one:

  • Two Nations open a trade route. What do they trade? Both gain the Relationship Trading with [Nation]. Name an Actor who protects or plunders it, with the Relationship Guardian of [Nation] or Raider of [Nation].
  • The few grow rich at the expense of the many. How? Name an Actor of a Nation and give them the Quality Wealthy. The Nation gains the Relationship Extracted by [Actor].
9♠

Nine of SpadesHunted

Choose one:

  • A Nation turns on a People within its borders. Who does it blame, and for what? Give the People the Relationship Persecuted by [Nation]. Give the Nation a Journey to drive the People out. If it succeeds, then in addition to its outcomes, the Nation gains the Relationship Exiled [People].
  • A price is put on someone's head. Whose, and who wants them? Name or select that Actor and give them the Quality Wanted and a negative Quality. Give another Actor a Journey to slay or capture them.
10♠

Ten of SpadesDigging Up the Past

Someone looks to the past. What are they searching for, and why now? Select an Actor or Nation to be the seeker, and an Entity from a prior Era or one removed from play. Give the seeker the Relationship Seeking [Entity] and a Journey to find it.

J♠

Jack of SpadesGuest Right

Choose one:

  • A Nation opens its doors. Select two Peoples from beyond its borders. They settle in it. Give the Nation a Quality that shows it (e.g. Multicultural, Hospitable, Cosmopolitan), and each People a positive Quality.
  • Old wounds heal. Select one or more negative Relationships between two Entities, and replace them with positive ones. Name an Actor who bound them together, with a Relationship to each (e.g. Child of [Actor], Envoy of [Nation]).
Q♠

Queen of SpadesBig Talker

An Actor makes a public vow to do the impossible. What do they swear to achieve, and who heard them? Name or select that Actor and give them a Quality that shows their nerve (e.g. Daring, Resolute, Delusional) and an epic Journey to fulfill their vow.

K♠

King of SpadesThe Lost Heir

An heir to a fallen Nation steps forward. What proves their claim? Select a Nation removed from play. Name or select an Actor and give them the Relationship Heir of [Nation] and a long or epic Journey to reclaim it. If the Journey succeeds, use 2 of its outcomes to return the Nation to play and redraw its borders.

A♠

Ace of SpadesOnce Upon a Time

The time of the tale is beginning! Draw and resolve one more card, if any remain, then the game ends and the adventure begins. Journeys still in progress do not complete. They become the open threads of your tale, waiting for heroes to finish them.

If this is the first card drawn this Era, shuffle it into the deck and draw another.

★ Extra Cards

The following are extra cards that may appear in any Era.

🃏

Black Joker

Fate repeats itself. Replay the previously played Era card. If the card offered a choice, you must choose a different option.

🃏

Red Joker

The winds of fate blow in a random direction. Take another action. If you Name, you may Name any Entity, even one this Era doesn't allow.
